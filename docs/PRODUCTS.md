# Products, materials and protocols

**Admin → Producten & materialen.** Internal register of the products the owner actually uses: brand, variant, category, supplier, purchase price, stock, reorder level, batch/lot, expiry date, verified usage notes and a "professional use only" flag. Stock changes are recorded as movements (purchase, used, correction, expired) and the product stock is updated in the same database transaction. The page warns about products that are out of stock, at the reorder level, expired or expiring within 30 days.

**Protocols.** Per treatment, an internal step-by-step protocol with the products used in each step. Only visible to the owner.

**Public page.** `/producten` and `/en/products` are **off by default** (Admin → Producten & materialen → Publieke pagina). When switched on, only products marked "tonen" are listed — without prices, ordering or a shop.

## Nothing is pre-filled

No products, usage instructions or brand partnerships are invented. The owner mentioned using Depilève and Extenso products; both names are offered as suggestions in the brand field, nothing more.

### Research note (1 November 2026)

They describe a professional waxing range (strip, hot and film waxes, pre- and post-wax care) for salon use. The brand's own website could not be confirmed from those results, and the retailers disagree on its founding year — so no product facts are taken over into the site.
- **Extenso** — no official or reliable source was found in this search.

**Action for the owner:** enter the products you actually use from their packaging and the supplier's product information sheet. Mark professional-only products as such, so they are never presented as home-care advice.
